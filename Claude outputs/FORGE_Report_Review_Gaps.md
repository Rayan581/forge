# FORGE report review: gaps, inconsistencies and weak spots

Reviewed 24 Nov 2026. Read only; no files were changed.

**What was reviewed:** the LaTeX source in `docs/reports/mid_report/latex/` (all chapters, appendices and `references.bib`). It was checked against:
- the current prototype in `godot_prototype/` and `ml_spike/`
- `results_sim_v3.md` and `ml_spike/judge/results_judge.md`
- `FORGE_Final1_Session_Summary.md`, `TODO.md` and `CHANGES.md`
- the committee feedback, the FYP-1 Mid rubric (Forms 2 and 3) and the FYP-1 Final expectations in `FYP_Orientation_Notes.md` (page 4 and page 6)

**Headline:** the report is still the Mid-1 report (dated 15 Oct). The prototype has moved well past it. Most of its technical claims about the action space, enemies, telemetry, reward and policy results are now out of date. For FYP-1 Final it needs a new iteration chapter, a results chapter against baselines, and several sections the Final gate asks for that the report doesn't have yet.

Findings are grouped by priority. File references point to `docs/reports/mid_report/latex/chapters/`.

---

## A. Out of date compared with the current prototype (fix first)

1. **Action space is 8-D, not 7-D.** `ai/policy_weights.json` has output layer shape 8×64 (`[health, damage, speed, aggro, hazard, scarcity, room_count, archetype_complexity]`). The report says 7-D everywhere: abstract, exec summary, Ch1 (OBJ-2, Module 2), Ch2 comparison table and §Proposed Solution, FR-04, FR-07, Ch5 action contract and Table `param_ranges`, Decision 1 (`8→64→64→7`), TC-03, and the conclusion. `a7` and the archetype-mix formula (`DifficultyManager.compute_archetype_mix`) are not documented anywhere.
2. **Five enemy archetypes, not two.** Brute, Skirmisher and Swarmer exist in `Enemy.gd` (with `SlamShockwave.gd`) and have tests (`test_enemy_archetypes`, `test_archetype_mix`, `test_swarm_room`). The report still describes them as future work: FR-08, UC-02, Ch5 components, the enemy state diagram, Ch6 §Multi-Archetype, Limitation 1, Ch10 §5-Archetype and the scope matrix.
3. **Calibration floors changed.** Ch6 §Cold-Start says Floor 2 has "35% Pyromancers" and Floor 3 has "Pyromancers only". The code now uses mixes `[0.5 crawler, 0.5 swarmer]` for Floor 2 and `[0.75 pyro, 0.25 skirmisher]` for Floor 3. Floor 5 now includes 30% Brutes, and every calibration floor has its own `aggro`, `scarcity` and `count_scale`.
4. **Telemetry is SQLite now, not a flat JSON file.** The game writes `user://forge_telemetry.db` (with `schema.sql`, `queries.sql`, a `v_corpus` view and schema migrations) and falls back to JSON only if the godot-sqlite plugin is missing. Still describing JSON: FR-03, UC-01 postcondition, Ch5 §Data Design (Table `json_schema`), Ch6 demo evidence, Ch1 and Ch9 privacy sections, Limitation 3, and the contribution table ("JSON serialization").
5. **The reward in Eq. `reward_function` (Ch5) is the v1 reward.** The sim-v3 reward in `train_ppo_difficulty.py` (`reward_fn`, around line 571) adds a flow/damage-ratio term, a room count judged against *skill* rather than challenge, an archetype-complexity term, the hit-severity and cover priors, a balance prior and a smoothness penalty. The simulator also gained reflex and tactics axes and abrupt skill shifts. None of this is in the report.
6. **All policy results in Ch8 come from the old policy.** Training curves (300k steps), Table `policy_eval`, Table `policy_params` ("damage stays at its minimum, speed at its maximum, aggro and scarcity untrained"), the Floor 6 cold-start outcomes (HP 20/20/120) and edge-case Scenarios 2 and 3 all predate sim-v3. The shipped policy now comes from 3 seeds × 1M steps. It passes every sim-v3 criterion and beats random-init, the heuristic and static +8% on 6 of 6 profiles. The v3 figures already exist in `ml_spike/analysis/figures/*_v3.png`.
7. **The abstract, exec summary and conclusion now undersell the current state.** They say the policy "personalizes only part of the 7-dimensional action space" and that comparisons with the heuristic and static baselines are "scheduled". Both have been addressed in simulation.
8. **A Judge model now exists but is described as a plan.** Ch10 and Limitation 2 call it a planned LightGBM model on a 25-session human corpus with live TreeSHAP in Godot. What was actually built: LightGBM trained on **30,199 synthetic sim-v3 floors**, test MAE 0.052 against 0.104 for the rescaled proxy, offline TreeSHAP and Design Insight Reports. It was validated on **29 real floors from one player**, and on those floors it does *not* beat the proxy (MAE 0.311 against 0.162 versus ratings). The GDScript TreeSHAP port moved to FYP-2. The report has to say this plainly, and the plan changes need change-control entries (see B6).
9. **New playtest features are missing.** Consent screen, post-floor 1–5 difficulty rating, pause menu, playtest/dev build split, and `install_id` (`ConsentScreen.gd`, `FloorRating.gd`, `PauseMenu.gd`, `GameSettings.gd`, `BuildInfo.gd`) appear in no use case, FR, diagram or test. The 18 Nov supervisor request (consent before the difficulty screen) also needs to be recorded.
10. **Map generation changed.** Ch5 still says the exit is the "Manhattan-farthest room" and rooms have a fixed size. The code picks the exit at the maximum BFS door depth (`DungeonGenerator.gd:85`), sizes rooms per room, adds corridors per door, and no longer tints whole rooms.
11. **Art pipeline changed.** Ch6 lists Pillow-generated 64×64 sprites (`player.png`, `enemy_crawler.png`, …), and the dependency table lists Pillow and `generate_prototype_sprites.py`. The game now uses the downloaded asset packs (skeleton player and crawler, priest pyromancer, CC0 0x72 art for the new enemies) through `tools/build_sprite_frames.py`.
12. **Limitations 6 and 7 are partly resolved.** `ranged_enemy_ratio` now comes from `a7` (`DifficultyManager.gd:51`), which the simulator models. Frame times are now logged per floor (`frame_avg_ms`, `frame_p95_ms`, `frame_max_ms` in `schema.sql`), so NFR-05 and SC-03 can be reported from the playtest data instead of "not yet measured".
13. **The Ch8 test suite and defect log are incomplete.** The report lists TC-01 to TC-10 and three CI tests. There are now 16 headless Godot test scripts (`test_overlay_sync`, `test_playtest_flow`, `test_telemetry_sqlite`, `test_telemetry_fallback`, `test_archetype_mix`, …) and 145 pytest tests (including `test_sim_v3` and `test_judge`). The DefenseOverlay slider re-emit bug ("only HP appeared to adapt") should be added as DEF-10. The Appendix CI listing also lists only three GDScript tests.
14. **Every UML and design figure shows the Mid-1 system.** That covers the use case diagram, class diagram, scene tree, activity diagram, sequence diagram, enemy state machine (2 archetypes), two-stage pipeline and low-level architecture. The class diagram is missing `TelemetryBackend`, `SqliteBackend`, `JsonBackend`, `ConsentScreen`, `FloorRating`, `PauseMenu`, `GameSettings` and `SlamShockwave`. Rubric Form 2 criterion 5 caps marks when diagrams don't match the code.

## B. Missing sections (FYP-1 Final gate and rubric)

1. **No Iteration 3 chapter.** Ch6 covers Iterations 1–2 only. Final-1 needs an iteration chapter for Weeks 11–14 covering archetypes, SQLite, map generation, assets, sim-v3, Judge, consent and rating.
2. **No measured comparison against a baseline.** The orientation notes require "Comparison baseline measured" at FYP-1 Final, and directive 9 is still only partly met. The BSP generator doesn't exist yet (no BSP code in `scripts/`), and there are no layout-diversity metrics. The sim-v3 tables are a simulated comparison against static +8%, and that should become its own results section.
3. **No ERD or schema diagram.** Form 3 criterion 6 expects ERD, schema or data-flow diagrams. The SQLite `sessions`/`floors` schema and the `v_corpus` view need one; the report only has a JSON snapshot table.
4. **No deployment section.** The orientation notes ask for deployment topology, a first deployment attempt, and a test, deployment, rollback and handover plan. A Windows playtest build exists (`dist/FORGE.exe`, `export_presets.cfg`, `release.yml`, build 0.9.0), so it can be reported. Ch10 also promises Linux and WebAssembly builds, but only Windows presets exist, and the Web build would need the godot-sqlite wasm path tested.
5. **No document control.** The orientation notes (page 6) require version, date, author and status on every document, and versioning instead of overwriting. The report has no version block, and the Final-1 report would currently overwrite the Mid-1 one in the same folder.
6. **Change control isn't reflected.** `CHANGES.md` stops at CR-03 (September), and the report never references it. Needed entries: 8-D action space, sim-v2 and sim-v3 reward redesign, SQLite schema, Judge trained on synthetic data instead of a 25-human corpus, TreeSHAP-in-Godot moved to FYP-2, consent and rating UI added (18 Nov), Brutes on calibration Floor 5, and the asset-pack swap.
7. **Requirements aren't prioritized.** The orientation notes say requirements should be uniquely identified, testable *and prioritized*. FRs and NFRs have no priority (e.g. MoSCoW). There are also no NFRs for maintainability or portability, while the notes ask for a measurable NFR per quality attribute.
8. **The committee's acceptance criteria are still undefined.** Convergence time, layout diversity and adaptation stability are missing (directive 5 is marked "partially completed"). The data already exists for two of them: the sim-v3 abrupt-shift test (recovers in 2 floors) and mean |Δaction| (0.058 against the 0.35 limit). Layout diversity needs the BSP or diversity script. SC-07 (DER ≤ 0.15) now passes for every simulated profile (0.12–0.13), not only novices.
9. **The meeting log stops at 15 Oct.** Meetings after Mid-1, including the 18 Nov supervisor meeting from `TODO.md`, are missing.
10. **There's no plan to recruit 25+ players for the Judge.** The 10-person UAT is described, but the Judge corpus needs 25+ consented players, and the report doesn't say how they will be recruited, when, or whether they are the same people as the UAT group.

## C. Inconsistencies and errors in the current text

1. **The Godot version varies.** The Authors' Declaration says "Godot Engine 4.3". Ch1, Ch6 and the dependency table say 4.7.2. `README.md` says "CI pins 4.3-stable". AI log AI-001 says "Godot 4.7.1", and commit `4e46fbc` mentions 4.3 checksums. Pick the true version and use it everywhere.
2. **The AI disclosure doesn't match the AI log.** The report says AI-002 used "Claude 3.7 Sonnet" and that the log "currently holds two entries". `AI logs.xlsx` holds **one** entry (AI-001). AI-002 is only in `entry_audit_fixes.json`, which says "Claude 4.6 Sonnet" and "Godot 4.3".
3. **Xue et al. 2017 appears twice in Ch2,** under Generation 2 and again under Generation 3 "Deep Reinforcement Learning", even though it isn't an RL method. Earle 2021 (quality-diversity, evolutionary) is also in the RL list. The text admits this, but the grouping still reads as padding.
4. **The Hades God Mode figures (20% resistance, +2% per death, up to 80%) have no citation.**
5. **14 of the 40 bib entries are `@misc`,** and several are arXiv versions of papers that were later published (e.g. EDRL at IEEE CoG 2021, "Truly PPO" at UAI 2019). Citing the published versions strengthens the "40 sources" claim.
6. **The effort split doesn't match the evidence.** Every member is listed at 33.3%, but Uwaid has no commit in either commit table and Rayan authored 9 of 11 listed commits. The authorship note helps, but Form 2 criterion 6 ("history dominated by a single member") and Form 11 are triggered by exactly this pattern.
7. **The privacy section is now inaccurate.** It claims no personal identifiers and no data leaving the machine. The game now stores a persistent random `install_id`, which is a pseudonymous identifier, and testers send their `forge_telemetry.db` to the team (`PLAYTEST.md` §3). Those are reasonable choices, but the report has to describe them: consent wording, withdrawal and deletion, who holds the files, how long they are kept, and whether university ethics approval is needed.
8. **Asset licences haven't been verified.** `assets/sprites/CREDITS.md` says the two user-supplied packs are under "licence as supplied … not re-checked". The dependency register lists neither pack, nor godot-sqlite (MIT), LightGBM, SHAP, NumPy, SciPy or pandas.
9. **The cover-page dates need updating for Final-1:** submission date 15 Oct 2026, `\degreemonth{October}`, and the declaration's "Iterations 1 and 2 (Weeks 1 through 10)".

## D. Weak spots the panel is likely to probe

1. **Part of the personalization is built into the reward.** The sim-v3 reward sets the room-count and complexity targets from the simulated player's *hidden* `skill`, and the severity and cover priors from hidden `reflex`/`tactics` (`reward_fn`). So "Spearman(skill, challenge) = 1.000" partly follows from the design. `results_sim_v3.md` states this honestly; the report needs to as well, and should separate what the policy learned from what the priors imposed.
2. **The static +8% baseline is weak.** It kills novices (0.7 deaths per floor) and was never tuned, so beating it says little. The committee asked for a *strong* non-adaptive baseline. A cheap, convincing option is a "best fixed setting" tuned on the whole simulated population (the constant-action optimizer is already mentioned in `results_sim_v3.md`), alongside the BSP generator.
3. **The Judge still doesn't meet directive 8** ("validate using empirical player data"). It is trained on synthetic data, and on the only real data (one player, 29 floors) it loses to the hand-weighted proxy and under-predicts by about 0.10. Presenting this as an honest sim-to-real finding is fine. Calling the directive "completed" would not be.
4. **All PPO evidence is still simulation-only.** No human-play comparison exists yet, and the pace target (7.5 s per room) is still uncalibrated against real play, even though 29 real floors are available to check it.
5. **There is still no rate limiter.** Smoothness now comes only from a reward penalty. Ch2 and Ch9 promise a limiter for FYP-2. Either use the measured |Δa| to argue none is needed, or schedule it.
6. **The PPO justification is argued, not tested.** The PPO vs SAC/DQN/bandit table is qualitative. One SAC run in the same environment would be cheap and would answer "why PPO" with data. The committee also asked for "recent RL techniques", and the table covers only established methods.
7. **The player-measures synthesis is missing.** Ch2 lists GEQ and PENS but never maps established performance, engagement, satisfaction and difficulty measures onto FORGE's player model and evaluation, which the committee asked for. The 1–5 perceived-difficulty rating now exists and should be tied back to that literature.
8. **The AI-use disclosure is the biggest integrity risk.** The orientation notes allow AI "only for localized assistance". The report already admits that AI-001 generated the first prototype. Since Mid-1, most Final-1 features (archetypes, the SQLite merge, sim-v3, Judge, consent UI) were built in Claude sessions, as `FORGE_Final1_Session_Summary.md` and the hand-off brief show. That has to be in the AI log and the disclosure section. Every owner should also be ready for the "sudden code volume" red-flag walkthrough, because the "all members fully capable of modifying any module live" claim will be tested.
9. **Calibration floors can confound the comparisons.** The adaptive condition gets 5 fixed calibration floors that the static and BSP conditions don't. The UAT and simulated comparisons should either give all conditions the same first 5 floors or report floors 6+ separately.
